Delete Your Account

NSDI ChocoCravings

If you would like to delete your NSDI ChocoCravings account and associated data, you can request this by contacting us directly.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to nsdi.chococravings2k24@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line:

"Account Deletion Request"

Please include your registered phone number or email so we can locate your account. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your name, email, and phone number
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Account login credentials
  • Loyalty tier and rewards data

What May Be Retained

  • Order history may be retained for up to 1 year for accounting, tax, and legal compliance purposes, in anonymized or aggregated form where possible
  • Payment transaction records may be retained as required by Indian financial regulations

Requesting Deletion of Specific Data Only

If you'd like only certain data deleted (for example, your saved address) without deleting your entire account, mention this in your email request and we will action it accordingly.
